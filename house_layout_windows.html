Key design decisions:
• Second Drawing Room replaced by Garage — added on west side near main gate, which is now correctly on the 45 ft west wall.
• One Sky Open only — placed above TV Lounge to serve as the single internal light well. No other sky openings.
• All 3 bathrooms on east exterior wall — Bath 1, 2 & 3 each get a frosted ventilation window on the east side. Bath 1 also gets a south window.
• Bed 1 faces south — receives direct sunlight and full south wall window. Beds 2 & 3 use the Bed Hall corridor with a skylight for indirect natural light — a common and practical solution in compact layouts.
• Kitchen on south wall — ideal casement window for ventilation while cooking.
• Drawing Room — opens toward the Lawn garden (west-facing), creating a pleasant indoor-outdoor connection without needing a north window.
