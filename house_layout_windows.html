Window Placement Summary

  • Drawing Room (upper): Full-width window on north exterior wall for maximum light.
  • Drawing Room (lower): Window on south wall facing the open space 4.5x105.
  • TV Lounge: No exterior wall — glazed internal opening toward Sky Open (4x20) for daylight.
  • Sky Open: Keep fully open-to-sky; acts as light well for TV Lounge and Bed 3.
  • Bed 3: Window on north exterior wall; also benefits from adjacency to Sky Open.
  • Bed 2: Window on east wall facing Open Space 33x10 — good morning light.
  • Bed 1: Two windows — east wall and south wall, both facing open spaces.
  • Bath 1: Small frosted window on east wall (shared wall with Bed 2 side — check partition).
  • Bath 2: Small frosted top-hung window on east exterior wall.
  • Bath 3: Small frosted window on north exterior wall near ceiling level.
  • Kitchen: Casement window on south wall above counter, facing open space — ideal ventilation.
  • Study: Internal glass panel or borrowed-light opening toward Kitchen for daylight.
  • Corridor: Skylight above — most practical solution for an interior corridor.
  • Stair & Powder Room: Small window on south wall + skylight over stairwell.
  • Lawn: Grille or decorative opening on west boundary wall for air circulation and view.
