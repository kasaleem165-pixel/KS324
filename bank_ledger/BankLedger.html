Create Memorandum Account
A memorandum account is an internal sub-ledger — e.g. "Loan to Ali", "Advance Received – Zubair", "Petty Cash Custody". Tag any Main Entry to one and it automatically posts into that account's own ledger too.
Enter as a natural balance for the nature chosen above.
Record Memo Entry
Post an entry directly into a memorandum account — for things with no bank transaction of their own, e.g. an accrual, a write-off, or correcting a past balance. This posts to the account's ledger only; it never touches any bank balance. To record something that also moved money through a bank, use Main Entry instead and tag it to the memo account there.
