A. Introduction & Background

The internal audit report is submitted as a result of audit exercise of Girls Campus Officers Colony, Multan. The audit was performed as Routine Audit Visit along with follow-up of last audit report. The period covered by this report is from 08 Sep, 2020 to 23, Feb 2026.

Initial findings were discussed with concerned Branch Head. Other officials, at all levels of organizational hierarchy, were also consulted where necessary. Audit team visited the Branch from 23 Feb to 06 Mar, 2026.

B. Objectives & Scope

Purpose of the visit was routine branch audit along with follow up of last audit report. Main objectives of this exercise included:

  • Correctness, completeness, timeliness, authorization and integrity of data entry in financial system
  • Review of general compliance of accounting practices and framework
  • Compliance with Company Policies and Manuals
  • Review of controls over Cash and Bank
  • Review of system for recording and safeguarding fixed assets
  • Review of procedures related to procurement and imprest adjustments/reimbursements
  • Review of employee and student records for completeness and authenticity
  • Review of library record and related documentation
  • Compliance with Emergency Procedures and security parameters

C. Audit Methodology

In order to achieve the aforesaid objectives, audit areas were planned on the basis of initial risk assessment. A representative sample was selected from relevant records for performing various audit tests. The audit findings were analysed according to the Internal Control – Integrated Framework published by COSO.

The audit observations were discussed with Branch Head, other auditee staff and concerned staff of Regional Office (RO). Their responses are included in this report where appropriate.
