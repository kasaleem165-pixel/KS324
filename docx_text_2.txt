Introduction and Background
This internal audit report is submitted after audit exercise of BCP Qasimabad, Hyderabad. The audit was performed as routine audit visit. The period covered by this report is from 31 May, 2022 to 8 Feb, 2026. Initial findings were discussed with concerned Branch Head. Other officials, at all levels of organizational hierarchy, were also consulted where necessary. Audit team visited the branch from 9 Feb to 18 Feb, 2026.
Objectives and Scope
Purpose of the visit was routine branch audit along with follow up of last audit report. The scope of this exercise had already been circulated to all the concerned through Audit Intimation Letter. Main objectives of this exercise included:
To check the correctness, completeness, timeliness, authorization and integrity of data entry in financial system and related documentation.
To review the compliance with accounting practices and framework.
To review the compliance with Company Policies and Manuals.
To review the controls over Cash and Bank.
To review the system for recording and safeguarding the fixed assets.
To review the procedures related to procurement imprest adjustments/reimbursements.
To review the employees and students’ records to ensure their completeness and authenticity.
Check student attendance registers along with other related record to validate the base record for revenue.
Review the library record and related documentation with respect to recording and existence.
Check and evaluate the compliance with “Emergency Procedures” and the specified security parameters which have been adopted in this regard.
To check stock register along with related record (if any).
Review of third party contracts and their compliance.
Audit Methodology
In order to achieve the aforesaid objectives under specific circumstances of the Branch and relevant Region, the audit areas and various exercises were planned on the basis of initial risk assessment. A representative sample, where necessary, was selected from relevant records for performing various audit tests. Due to inherent limitations and sample based nature of audit, there exists a possibility that some issues/problems may remain undetected. In a few instances where the data was not available or was in a form that could not be used for audit purposes, general observation based analytical techniques and questioning methods were applied.
The audit findings were analysed according to vastly accepted Internal Control – Integrated Framework published by COSO. It is pertinent to mention here that except for the country specific legal requirements; COSO Framework is the most customary framework for preferred phase-compliance of Sarbanes-Oxley Act (SOX).
The audit observations were discussed with concerned Branch Head, other auditee staff and concerned staff of Cluster Support Office.  Their response is also included in this report where appropriate. Immediate actions were ensured to be taken where required in order to enhance the effectiveness of this internal audit exercise.

Detailed Audit Observations and Findings
More specific (micro level) audit findings, along-with recommendations thereon, are as follows:
Repeated Observations (Un-Resolved)
Repeated observations from the last audit report were those for which corrective measures were not taken by the concerned management, and it indicates absence of concern over matters highlighted.
Timely efforts and implementation of recommendations mentioned in the Audit reports can ensure the avoidance of repetitive observations.
Branch Imprest, Payments and Accounts
Withholding Tax Deposited with Delay
Tax withheld from the suppliers should be deposited within seven days from the date of payment to suppliers/vendors as per company policy. Delays were observed in deposit of withholding tax deducted on payments to suppliers/vendors. Detail of instances are given as Annexure-A.
Recommendation
Company policies should be strictly followed; any lapses in this regard may result in penalties to the company.
As per Manager Financial Operations (BSS-HO) letter dated 01 Jul, 2019;
“Follow the instructions at your end and also instruct the schools and other concerned staff to deduct the tax accordingly and deposit the same into the Government Treasury within seven (7) days from the date of deduction/payment.”
As per “Audit & Financial Control, 5. Payment of Invoices” Clause of INDEX;
“Branch Accountant has to ensure that Withholding Tax is deposited within the stipulated time.”
Auditee Response
In the future, we will make our best efforts to deposit the taxwithin seven days. However, on several occasions, the DIB system was not functioning properly, which resulted in delays in timely tax deposits.

Chart of Account Needs Correction
Sub accounts should be opened in their respective parent account categories. During audit it was observed that sub accounts were opened in inappropriate parent category of “Advance to Suppliers”. Instances are mentioned as Annexure-B.
Recommendation
Clarity of the Books of Accounts wholly depends upon true classification of Chart of Accounts. To present fair view of accounts the concerned heads should be revised and transferred to related parent head category.
Referred to Head of Finance
Auditee Response
We will seek advice from the Regional Office (RO) to resolve this issue.
RO Response
Accountants at branch level only have rights to open accounts in Advance to Supplier, Banks and Advance to Staff. They do not have rights to open accounts in any other category.
Audit View
RO finance dept. should consult HO finance /IT dept. for the resolution of constraint.
Vendors Registration Forms not Available
Vendor Registration Application (VRA) Forms should be available in the branch for verification and to identify the regular vendors of branch. Whereas, it was observed that (VRA) Vendors Registration Process was not conducted for some vendors. Details mentioned as Annexure-C.
Recommendation
Forms of Vendor’s Registration should be available filled completely and carefully. According to “Procurement, 6. Sourcing, 2. Registration of Vendors”, clause of the INDEX;
“The designated SC Official / AMCO shall ensure that the particulars of vendor have been entered in Vendor Database / Register as registered vendor and notify the vendor thereof in writing.”
“BH shall review and ensure periodically that the Vendor Database / Register is being maintained and updated effectively on regular basis. Such review shall be conducted at least once in a calendar year.”
Auditee Response
Observation noted. The remaining VRA will be completed shortly.
Deficiencies in Vendors Registration Applications (VRA)
VRA’s should be properly & filled and enclosed with all the supporting documents. Whereas it was observed that some VRAs were not supported with some relevant documents. Instances are mentioned as Annexure-D.
Recommendation
Forms of Vendor’s Registration should be filled completely and carefully, and, should be duly approved by the branch head. As per “Procurement, 6. Sourcing, 2. Registration of Vendors” clause of INDEX;
“The designated BPC shall complete the VRF, based on the criteria detailed above and sign the completed form. VRF and related documents, if any, shall be forwarded to BH for approval.”
“BH shall review the documents submitted and provide approval on VRF for inclusion in Vendor Database / Register, upon satisfaction.”
Auditee Response
Observation noted. The remaining VRA supporting documents will be completed shortly.
Quality and Brand not Mentioned for Annual Paint Work
Due diligence in comparison of contractor’s quotations should be performed. Following discrepancies were observed during checking of annual paint work transaction 2025-26;
Quality & Brand of Paint Material not mentioned on all of the Quotations, Comparative Statement as well as on Final Invoice of the contractors.
Quotations were not collected and compared in true sense, as, following technicalities were not clearly mentioned on all of the quotations, which reveals that these aspects were not considered at the time of quotations review, preparation of comparative statements and awarding the contract;
Pre-Scrapping of Existing Paint.
Number of Paint Coats Application (Single/Double).
Brands of Paint Material not matching in Quotations.
Recommendation
Core objective to obtain these quotations is to get fair market analysis, which apparently, was not achieved while executing these transactions.
Improper quotation and invoices should have been identified and rectified by the branch officials at the time of checking/review of bills and quotations.
Auditee Response
Observation noted. In future, we will obtain proper quotations for paint, including detailed specifications.
Delay in Claiming Reimbursement against Expenses
Branch is given monthly imprest budget for its routine expenses, any delay in claiming reimbursements may lead to shortage of funds for routine expenses. It was observed that RO reimbursement claims were forwarded with delay by the branch. Instances are given as Annexure-E.
Recommendation
Branch should process reimbursement claims without any delay. As per “Audit & Financial Control, 5. Reimbursement Process, 4. Reimbursement Claim Procedure”, Clause # 1 of INDEX;
”After incurring expenditure approved as per Approval Of Activity/Expenditure, the reimbursement claim along with related documents/supports shall be forwarded to the Office responsible to reimburse the amount within 10 days of completion of the activity.”
Auditee Response
Observation noted. In  future, we will make our best efforts to submit reimbursement claims to the Regional Office (RO) in a timely manner.
Recruitment, Payroll and Employee Record
Deficiencies in Employee Files
Employee files should be complete in all respects. While checking other files, it was observed that documents like Nomination Forms, photographs, emergency contact, next to kin information were missing in the files, whereas Security Clearance Forms were also found incomplete in some cases. Few of the instances are given in Annexure-F.
Recommendation
All the employees’ files should be complete and updated. According to “Human Resource, 32. Employee Files”, Clause of the INDEX;
“Files of all School employees shall be maintained and updated properly.”
“Branch Head will ensure that all the required documents are filed in personal files of employees.”
Auditee Response
Missing documentation is being under completion to ensure full compliance.
Audit View
Missing documents are being collected and employee files are being updated accordingly.
Short Office Hours of Teaching Staff
Branch Head should ensure that standard office hours are being followed by employees. During sample-based review of employees’ attendance, it was observed that some of the Branch staff was not following the official timings. The matter may affect operational efficiency of the Branch and the leave encashment and salary of respective staff. The attendance from Jun-2022 to Feb-2026 was checked and instances of short office hours have been mentioned in Annexure-G.
Recommendation
Branch Head should monitor the working hours of the subordinate staff and in case of nonconformity, necessary steps should be taken to maintain discipline. In case of early departure, short leave should be applied. As per “Human Resource, 16. Working Hours & Attendance, 2. Working Hours”, Clause of INDEX;
Branch staff shall report for duty as per the following table:
Position
Duty Times
Teachers on duty
30 minutes Before School Starts
30 minutes After School Ends
Other teaching staff
15 minutes Before School Starts
15 minutes After School Ends
Auditee Response
The matter has been noted. Teaching staff have been instructed to strictly follow the prescribed office timings. Attendance will be monitored regularly to ensure compliance with the policy.
Audit View
In cases of early time out, relevant staff should be asked to apply for “Early Leaving Application”. In cases of deliberate and habitual non-compliance, appropriate action should be taken by the HR department.
Outgoing Attendance not Marked by Staff
Attendance should be regularly marked by all the employees. It was noted that outgoing attendance of few employees was not marked in some cases. The matter may affect the leave balance and encashment of the same. Instances are mentioned as Annexure-H.
Recommendation
Branch Head should monitor the attendance record of the Branch employees and in case of attendance not marked, staff should raise application highlighting the reasons. As per  “Human Resource, 16. Working Hours & Attendance, 3. Attendance”, Clause of INDEX;
“Employees are expected to report to work as scheduled and on time. Employees shall mark their attendance on biometric devices (online attendance) installed in offices.”
Auditee Response
The matter has been noted and staff have been instructed to ensure proper marking of both incoming and outgoing attendance. Attendance will be monitored regularly to avoid such instances in the future.
Audit View
In case of “Employee Out” not marked, application for “Attendance not Marked” should be raised. In cases of deliberate and habitual non-compliance, appropriate action should be obtained by the HR department.
