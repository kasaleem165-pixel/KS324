# Beaconhouse Group — Internal Audit: Management Analysis Report
**Systems & Audit Department**
**Prepared: June 2026 | Scope: 8 Branches | Total Observations: 501**

---

## Executive Summary

An analysis of **501 audit observations** across **8 branches** (5 in Lahore, 2 in Karachi, 1 in Hyderabad) reveals systemic control weaknesses that extend beyond individual branches. Of particular concern:

- **36% of all observations are repeated** (180 of 501), indicating that prior audit findings are not being resolved — pointing to a structural compliance enforcement gap.
- **38 High-Risk and 164 Medium-to-High-Risk observations** (40% of total) require immediate attention.
- **39 distinct observations recur across 3 or more branches**, confirming these are organisation-wide issues requiring policy-level intervention, not just branch-level correction.

## 7. Key Recommendations for Management

### Immediate Actions (0–30 days)
1. **Forensic review** of procurement and cash handling at Valencia Town (217), LMA 11-FCC Gulberg, and 51 FCC — High-Risk financial integrity findings.
2. **Security remediation** at BCP Qasimabad — 6 High-Risk security findings including non-functional CCTV and missing safety equipment.
3. **Salary overpayment recovery** at Valencia Town (217) — excess salary paid to employee must be quantified and recovered.
4. **Canteen dues recovery** at Valencia Town (217) — contractor removed assets without settling dues.

### Systemic Fixes (30–90 days)
5. **BEAMS automation** — configure system alerts for: registration fee deposit deadlines, employee confirmation timelines, WHT deposit deadlines.
6. **Procurement controls** — enforce 3-vendor quotation policy with system-level controls; flag single-vendor quotations for RO approval.
7. **Vendor registration** — centralize and audit vendor registers across all branches; make it a mandatory pre-requisite before any payment.
8. **Employee file completeness** — launch a one-time file completion drive across all branches with a 60-day deadline monitored by HR-RO.

### Governance & Monitoring (Ongoing)
9. **Repeat observation escalation protocol** — any observation repeated for a second consecutive audit should automatically escalate to Regional Director level.
10. **Quarterly RO compliance reviews** — Regional Office to conduct formal mid-cycle compliance checks on all High and M-to-H risk observations.
11. **Branch Head KPI linkage** — tie audit observation repeat rates and resolution timelines to Branch Head performance evaluations.
12. **Regional Office accountability** — delayed reimbursement processing (a repeated finding across 4 branches) should be tracked and reported to Director Finance monthly.
