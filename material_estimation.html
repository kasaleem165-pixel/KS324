Engineering Assumptions

  • RCC framed structure with strip/pad footings. Concrete mix 1:2:4 for all structural concrete.
  • Brick mortar mix 1:6 (cement : sand). Plaster mix 1:4, thickness 0.5 inch.
  • Pakistani standard brick size: 9″ × 4.5″ × 3″. Bricks/sqft of face area: 9.5 (9″ wall), 4.75 (4.5″ wall).
  • Total foundation length (ext + int load-bearing walls): 400 running ft.
  • Wall lengths: G/F ≈ 550 R.ft, 1st Floor ≈ 380 R.ft (including internal subdivisions).
  • Door + window deduction: 20% of gross wall face area.
  • Slab thickness: 5 inches. Column size: 12″×12″. Beam size: 12″×15″.
  • Columns: G/F 15 nos, 1/F 12 nos. Beam runs: G/F 400 R.ft, 1/F 280 R.ft.
  • All quantities include 10% wastage in the Grand Summary.
