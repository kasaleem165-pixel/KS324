A. Introduction & Background

The internal audit report is submitted as a result of an audit exercise of 51 FCC Campus, Lahore. The audit was performed as a routine audit visit. The period covered by this report is from October 21, 2019 to January 26, 2026, while the audit visit took place from January 26, 2026 to February 18, 2026.

The audit was conducted by the Systems & Audit Department of Beaconhouse Group in accordance with the International Standards for the Professional Practice of Internal Auditing issued by the Institute of Internal Auditors (IIA).

↑ Back to Synopsis
B. Objectives & Scope

The purpose of the visit was a routine branch audit along with follow-up of the last audit report. The scope included:

  • ✔ Correctness & integrity of financial data entry
  • ✔ General compliance of accounting practices
  • ✔ Compliance with Company Policies and Manuals
  • ✔ Controls over Cash and Bank
  • ✔ Recording and safeguarding of fixed assets
  • ✔ Procurement and imprest reimbursements
  • ✔ Employee and student record completeness
  • ✔ Student attendance validation
  • ✔ Library records and documentation
  • ✔ Emergency Procedures and security compliance
  • ✔ Stock register and related records
  • ✔ Third-party contracts and their compliance
↑ Back to Synopsis
C. Audit Methodology

Audit areas were planned based on an initial risk assessment. Findings were analysed according to the widely accepted Internal Control — Integrated Framework (COSO). Country-specific legal requirements also governed the assessment.

Observations were discussed with the Branch Head, auditee staff, and concerned Regional Office staff. Their responses are included where appropriate. Immediate actions taken by the branch during the audit are also noted.

Section 1 — Repeated (Unresolved) Observations
1.1 Expenditure Exceeded the Budgeted Amount M to H
Branch Imprest, Payments & Accounts Referred To: BH Last Audit Ref: 1.4
Finding
Review of system-generated "Budget Variance Sheets" revealed significant overspending under various heads of accounts. Budget should be monitored by Branch Head and RO on a monthly basis.
Recommendation ▼
Overspending should be controlled/monitored by Branch Head. Imprest budget should be monitored by ROC on monthly basis. As per policy: "Budget variance shall be monitored for judicious use of funds allocated under all heads of expenses. Imprest budget variances of Branches shall be conducted monthly at the RO."
Auditee Response ▼
Due to the impact of hyperinflation, there have been discrepancies across a few heads of accounts. The school has consistently made every effort to manage and maintain expenditures within the allocated budget. Moving forward, every effort will be made to adhere strictly to the allocated budgets.
↑ Back to Synopsis
1.2Savings in Student ExpenditureL to M
Branch Imprest, Payments & AccountsReferred To: BHLast Audit Ref: 1.1
Finding
Budget for student expenditure remained under-utilized across multiple years. As per policy, there should be no saving of funds under the category of "student expenses".
Recommendation ▼
Budget for student expenses should be fully utilized. Savings in these heads should be avoided as per policy.
Auditee Response ▼
During 2019–20, 2020–21, and 2021–22 the school operated online due to COVID-19, restricting school events and activities. In 2022–23, budget was reserved for major events but deferred due to financial constraints. For 2023–24 and 2024–25, allocated budget for educational visits was insufficient; excursion trips were partially managed through cash collection from parents.
↑ Back to Synopsis
1.3Delay in Deposit of Withholding TaxM to H
Branch Imprest, Payments & AccountsReferred To: BHLast Audit Ref: 1.2
Finding
Withholding tax must be deposited within 7 days of deduction. Multiple delays were observed in tax deposits, creating risk of government penalties for the company.
Recommendation ▼
Tax policies must be strictly followed. Any lapses may result in penalties. Per DF letter No. 10597 dated Jan 27, 2011: tax must be deposited into Government Treasury within seven days of deduction.
Auditee Response ▼
The branch remains vigilant in ensuring withholding tax is deposited per prescribed policy. Acknowledged room for improvement; even greater diligence will be exercised going forward.
↑ Back to Synopsis
1.4Delayed Reimbursement of Claims by ROL to M
Branch Imprest, Payments & AccountsReferred To: ROLast Audit Ref: 1.3
Finding
Late reimbursements from RO lead to shortage of funds for smooth branch operations. Policy requires reimbursement claims to be processed within 20 days of receipt.
RO Response ▼
ROC Finance handles reimbursements systematically but cannot commit to a 20-day timeline due to: (1) bills requiring PO generation, (2) late submissions from branches, (3) competing priorities. The 20-day timeline is deemed insufficient in many practical scenarios.
Audit View
Timelines mentioned in policies must be followed. If not practicable, they should be formally amended through the proper procedure.

2.3All 3 Quotations Provided by the Selected VendorHigh
Procurement — Conflict of InterestReferred To: BH
Finding
During a review of Purchase Committee records, it was found that all three required quotations for a procurement were submitted by the same vendor (the ultimately selected vendor). This represents a serious conflict of interest and undermines the competitive procurement process.
Recommendation ▼
Quotations must always be obtained from three genuinely independent vendors. Any procurement where quotation integrity is compromised should be escalated to management. Strict adherence to Purchase Committee procedures is required.
Auditee Response ▼
The branch acknowledges the finding and will ensure independent quotations are obtained for future procurements.
