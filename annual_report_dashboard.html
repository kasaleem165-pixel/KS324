Notable Investigation Cases

🔴 ROS – KG 1 Campus, Jauhar (425) | Attendance Fraud & Asset Misuse

▼

Findings: Attendance fraud and assets misuse discovered during fact-finding exercise.

Outcomes: Resignation (4), Warning Letter (2), Letter of Advice (8), Recovery (6)

Recoveries: Rs. 421,888 from Head of School (false leaves) + Rs. 183,705 from 5 teachers (unregistered students) = Rs. 605,593 total

🔴 ROS – Newlands Karachi (458) | Fraud & Misappropriation in School Funds

▼

Findings: Cash misappropriation, vendor collusion, and fund misuse identified.

Outcomes: Resignation (2), Recovery (4), Warning Letter (1), Letter of Advice (1), Blacklisting (2)

Recoveries: Rs. 30,762 + Rs. 21,411 (Officer Accounts), Rs. 18,200 + Rs. 14,200 + Rs. 16,790 (M/s Nishat), Rs. 22,755 (M/s Aftab Sports), Rs. 49,959 (Office Attendant)

🔴 ROS – Main Campus Quetta (471) | Blank Letter Heads, Fake Vendors

▼

Findings: Blank letter heads/bills, high rates, and fake vendor collusion.

Outcomes: Resignation (2), Recovery (2), Warning Letter (2), Letter of Caution (1), Blacklisting (2)

Recoveries: Rs. 80,140 from Officer Accountant + Rs. 34,703 from Member PC

🔴 RON – BSS-320/F10-4 Islamabad | Fund Misuse & Record Manipulation

▼

Findings: Misuse of company funds through unposted vouchers, record manipulation, and collusion with a PC member.

Outcomes: Resignation (1), Recovery (1), Warning Letter (1)

Recovery: Rs. 390,204 from Muzaffar Hayat (Branch Accountant)
