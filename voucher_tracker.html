Voucher Status Meanings

  • In Hand — voucher received, not yet reviewed.
  • Sample Out — voucher pulled out for audit sampling / review.
  • Observation — an observation has been raised on the voucher and is awaiting reply.
  • Checked - In Hand — review complete, voucher retained.
  • Released — voucher cleared and returned.
  • Post Audit — flagged for post-audit follow-up.
  • Post Audit - In Hand — post-audit voucher retained for re-check.
  • Mudasir / Chugtai Sb — pending with the named reviewer for decision.
  • Cancel Payment — payment cancelled / voucher withdrawn.

Raising, Replying To & Re-referring an Observation

The Observations tab is where every audit query against a voucher is raised and tracked, modelled on the "Audit Observation / Receiving" memo: it captures who it was sent To (the voucher's company), a Reference No., the Voucher #, receiving and bill-forwarding dates, the amount involved, who checked it, and the observation text itself.

Every observation keeps its own Reply / Follow-up History. When the first reply comes in, record it directly against that entry. If the reply is incomplete or unsatisfactory, click + Re-refer (Request Another Reply) — this adds a new row referencing the same observation (numbered -R1, -R2, ...) so the observation can be sent back for a fresh response as many times as needed, with the full back-and-forth kept on one record.

OBS Code

OBS Code mirrors the audit's standard observation categories (2–9 below); selecting an Observation Type auto-fills the matching code, which can still be overridden.

  • 2 — PMC Discrepancies Regarding Vendor Registration, RFQs and Budgetary Matters
  • 3 — Timelines of Execution, Incomplete, Erroneous or Missing Supporting Data
  • 4 — Late Payments
  • 5 — Tax Related Issues
  • 6 — Expense Booked Against Wrong Head
  • 7 — Errors in Data Entry, Typing and Calculation
  • 8 — Discrepancies in Employee Advances and Allowances
  • 9 — Miscellaneous Observations

Deduction Reasons

Every observation that results in a financial impact records why the amount was reduced: a Quantity discrepancy, a Rate discrepancy, or Other reason (e.g. missing support, tax withholding, policy non-compliance). More than one reason may apply to the same observation.

Reference Number Format

Observation references follow MMYYNNNN, e.g. 07260001 = July 2026, observation #0001. Re-referrals of the same observation are suffixed, e.g. 07260001-R1.

Data storage

All data is saved locally in this browser (localStorage). Use Backup (JSON) regularly and Restore to move data between machines or browsers — clearing browser data will erase unsaved records.
