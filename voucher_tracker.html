Status Meanings

  • In Hand — voucher received, not yet reviewed.
  • Sample Out — voucher pulled out for audit sampling / review.
  • Observation — an observation has been raised on the voucher and is awaiting reply.
  • Checked - In Hand — review complete, voucher retained.
  • Released — voucher cleared and returned.
  • Post Audit — flagged for post-audit follow-up.
  • Post Audit - In Hand — post-audit voucher retained for re-check.
  • Mudasir / Chugtai Sb — pending with the named reviewer for decision.
  • Cancel Payment — payment cancelled / voucher withdrawn.

Deduction Reasons

Every observation that results in a financial impact records why the amount was reduced: a Quantity discrepancy, a Rate discrepancy, or Other reason (e.g. missing support, tax withholding, policy non-compliance). More than one reason may apply to the same observation.

Reference Number Format

Observation references follow MMYYNNNN, e.g. 07260001 = July 2026, observation #0001.

Data storage

All data is saved locally in this browser (localStorage). Use Backup (JSON) regularly and Restore to move data between machines or browsers — clearing browser data will erase unsaved records.
